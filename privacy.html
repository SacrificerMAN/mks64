Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how Saran Chess Academy handles the information you share when you enquire about or join our online chess coaching services.

Information you provide

When you book a demo or contact us, you may provide your name, WhatsApp number, optional email address, and learning goal. Please share only information needed for your enquiry.

How we store enquiries

When you submit the demo form, we store the details you provide together with the page and campaign source in a private Google Sheet used by Saran Chess Academy. We use this information only to arrange your demo, respond to your enquiry, and understand which campaigns are useful. We do not sell your personal information.

How we use information

We use your details to respond to your enquiry, schedule or discuss a free demo, provide coaching-related communication, and improve our service. We do not sell personal information.

WhatsApp and other services

When you choose to continue on WhatsApp, your messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own policies. Links to chess platforms or third-party services are also governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

Data retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was shared. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

Children and parents

Many learners are children. A parent or guardian should submit enquiries, communicate with us, and supervise a child’s participation where appropriate.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete your enquiry information by contacting us on WhatsApp. You can also choose not to provide optional details.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or services change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
