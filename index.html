FAQ

Common questions.

Who is this for?

Beginners building fundamentals, intermediate players stuck on a plateau, and competitive players preparing for tournaments.

Is training online or offline?

Sessions are primarily online so you can train from anywhere. Formats can be adjusted based on your location and preference.

Do I need a chess board?

A physical board helps, but online platforms (Chess.com / Lichess) work well for most training and analysis sessions.

How is this different from free videos?

You get a structured path, feedback on your own games, and coaching that connects concepts to real decisions over the board.

Can I prepare for tournaments?

Yes. Tournament preparation can include opening review, tactical readiness, opponent preparation and practical game planning.

What languages do you teach in?

Sessions are available in English and Hindi with clear, practical explanations.
