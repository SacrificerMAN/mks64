FAQ

Common questions

Is online coaching actually effective?

Yes — if sessions are structured. We focus on how you think in real games (time pressure, practical decisions), not only engine lines. Students get homework and game review between classes.

What age groups do you teach?

Kids who can sit through a focused session, teens, and adults. Beginner track works well for school-age starters; intermediate and 1:1 suit serious juniors and adults.

Hindi ya English — kaunsi language?

Both. Classes can be in English, Hindi, or mixed — whatever helps the student learn fastest.

Free demo me kya hota hai?

We understand your level and goal, explain the right program, and answer parent/student questions. No payment required for the demo.

Kitne sessions milte hain?

Depends on the plan (monthly/yearly and group vs 1:1). On the free demo we map a realistic weekly schedule so progress is consistent.

Refund / trial?

Start with the free demo so expectations are clear. Plan and fees are shared on WhatsApp after we understand your level and goals.
