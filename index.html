07 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is MKS64 for?

Students can start from beginner level and continue through intermediate and advanced training. The exact program depends on the student's goals and current playing level.

Is coaching online or offline?

Online and personalised coaching formats can be arranged. Availability depends on the selected program and schedule.

Do you analyse my own games?

Yes. Own-game analysis is a core part of practical improvement because it connects training concepts with real decisions made during your games.

Can I prepare for tournaments?

Yes. Tournament preparation can include opening review, tactical readiness, opponent preparation and practical game planning.
