Live courses · curriculum

Only what you can use in real games.

♟ Beginner

Solid foundation from zero — confidence on the board.

24 classes · 3 months2× / week
  • Board mastery — ranks, files, square names
  • Piece moves + notation — write your games
  • Attack & capture unprotected pieces
  • Check / escape · 4 ways to defend
  • Castling, promotion, en passant
  • Basic mates: 2R, Q, R · mate in 1 Core
  • Candidate moves (best 2–3) + short visualization Key skill
  • Game review habit — one lesson from every game
OutcomeFull games alone · fewer hanging pieces · school / online ready

♞ Intermediate

Tactics + first real rating climb.

48 classes · 6 months2× / week
  • Pin, fork, skewer, discovery Tactics
  • Knight fork · deflection · decoy
  • Opening principles · back-rank mates
  • Mate in 2 · spot the opponent’s threats first
  • Pawn endings · passed pawn · rule of the square
  • Rooks on the 7th · simplification
  • Candidate moves in middlegame Key skill
  • Visualization: 3–5 move lines
OutcomeStable rating · fewer blunders · club / online events

♜ Advanced

Calculation, structure, conversion.

48 classes · 6 months2× / week
  • Clearance, overload, zwischenzug
  • Attack on castled king · 7th/8th rank Core
  • Correct exchanges · short calculation
  • Open files, outposts, weak squares
  • Pawn structures: doubled, isolated, backward
  • Minority attack · forcing moves
  • Candidate moves under the clock Key skill
  • Visualization for tactics & endgames
OutcomeTournament readiness · rating jump · calm under pressure

♚ FIDE Rating

Rated path — openings locked, calculation board-free.

96 classes · 12 months2× / week
  • 4 openings locked: 2 White + 2 Black Core
  • Attacking the king & center
  • Prophylaxis · good vs bad bishop
  • Rook endings · bishop pair
  • Exploiting weaknesses · space advantage
  • Preventing counterplay · two weaknesses
  • Board-free visualization practice Key skill
  • Candidate moves: find → rank → choose
OutcomeFIDE path · district/state ready · calculate without board

Quick answers

How do online classes work?

Live video + shared board. Homework + game review. Classes in English.

Yes — the Beginner course starts from zero. Level check in the free demo.

How long for a FIDE rating path?

FIDE Rating course 96 classes / 12 months. Openings + calculation focus.

After the free demo on WhatsApp — plans are clear, no price pressure on the website.

Do you offer online chess coaching worldwide?

Yes. Students around the world join live 1:1 online chess classes. The free demo helps place each player in the right course and discuss a suitable class time.

Do you coach tournament and FIDE players?

Yes. The FIDE Rating course focuses on calculation, openings, endgames and practical tournament preparation.

Yes. Share your preferred days and times in the free demo form, and we will discuss a suitable live class slot.
