Frequently asked questions

What families usually ask before joining.

Does my child need a US Chess rating?

No. Unrated players can start in the Foundation course. A rating and tournament history simply help us place the student more precisely.

Can students join from anywhere in the USA?

Yes. Classes are online, and families from ET, CT, MT and PT can request a suitable group or alternate slot.

Which platforms are used?

Live teaching takes place on Zoom. Games and training activities use Lichess or Chess.com, depending on the group.

What happens in the free assessment?

We review the student's experience, solve a few positions, discuss goals and recommend the most suitable course.

Can a student join during the month?

Yes, when space and scheduling allow. We confirm the start date and any prorated amount before enrollment.

Are private 1:1 lessons available?

Yes. Families who need an individual schedule or personalized tournament plan can request 1:1 coaching during the assessment.
